FAQ

Questions

What is a Soul Pass?

A soulbound token (SBT) on HyperEVM. It stays in the wallet that minted it and cannot be sold or transferred. It proves you joined early.

Can I mint more than one pass?

Yes. Each pass has its own tasks, and you can mint every pass you qualify for. Your highest pass decides your access on Elysium.

How do invites work?

After you join, you get your own invite link. A friend counts once they join ELYSVORA through it with their wallet and X account. GTD needs 3 friends and OG needs 7.

How is the $KNTQ holding checked?

The site reads your $KNTQ balance on HyperEVM and its live price when you tap Verify. Keep the balance in the same wallet until the snapshot.

What does minting cost?

$0.66 worth of $KNTQ per pass, plus a small HYPE gas fee on HyperEVM.

Do I need HYPE for Elysium?

Yes. Elysium uses HYPE for gas, bridged 1:1 from HyperEVM.

Is ELYSVORA part of Kinetiq?

No. ELYSVORA is an independent community project built for the Kinetiq and Elysium ecosystem. It is not run or endorsed by Kinetiq.
