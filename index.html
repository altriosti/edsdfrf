What is an SBT

A pass that proves you, not a ticket to flip.

A soulbound token is an NFT that cannot leave the wallet that minted it. It works like a badge on your on-chain identity.

Regular NFT

  • Can be sold or sent to anyone
  • Bots and farmers can buy their way in
  • Proves who owns it today, not who was early

ELYSVORA Soul Pass

  • Bound to your wallet forever
  • Earned by tasks, $KNTQ holding and invites
  • Snapshot reads it to set your Elysium access

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is a Soul Pass?

A soulbound token on HyperEVM. It stays in the wallet that minted it and cannot be sold or transferred.

Can I mint more than one pass?

Yes, in order. Silver unlocks Gold, and Gold unlocks Diamond. Your highest pass decides your access on Elysium.

Do I repeat the X tasks for Gold and Diamond?

No. Follow, like, repost and comment count once. If you did them for Silver, Gold and Diamond skip them.

How do invites work?

After you join you get an invite link, and it is added to your Gold and Diamond posts automatically. A friend counts once they join with their wallet and X. Gold needs 3 friends and Diamond needs 7.

How is my $KNTQ checked?

The app reads your $KNTQ balance on HyperEVM and the live price when you tap Verify. Keep it in the same wallet until the snapshot.

What does minting cost?

$0.66 worth of $KNTQ per pass, plus a small HYPE gas fee on HyperEVM.

When does Elysium launch?

Kinetiq has announced Elysium mainnet for November 9, 2026. The snapshot happens before that.

Is ELYSVORA part of Kinetiq?

No. ELYSVORA is an independent community project built for the Kinetiq and Elysium ecosystem. It is not run or endorsed by Kinetiq.
